This Refund Policy applies to paid digital content offered for RACKET RPG, including Racket Coins ("RC") and virtual items or benefits purchased with them.

Nothing in this Policy removes mandatory rights provided by applicable consumer law.

## 1. Payment channel

FiveM-related paid content must be sold only through a payment channel authorized under the applicable Cfx.re/FiveM platform rules.

The payment provider shown during checkout may apply additional payment, billing and refund terms. Where its rules or mandatory law grant you stronger rights, those rights remain unaffected.

## 2. Digital delivery

RC and other digital benefits are generally delivered electronically after successful payment confirmation.

A purchase is considered fulfilled when the purchased RC or digital entitlement has been credited to the relevant RACKET account, unless the nature of the product requires another form of delivery.

## 3. When you may request a refund

You may contact us about a refund where, for example:
- you were charged more than once for the same intended purchase;
- payment completed but the purchased RC or entitlement was not delivered and we cannot resolve the issue within a reasonable time;
- the delivered amount materially differs from the purchased package;
- the transaction was unauthorized, subject to reasonable account and payment verification;
- applicable law gives you a right to a refund, price reduction, withdrawal or another remedy.

Send requests to **support@racket.cat** and include your RACKET username, transaction reference and a brief explanation. Do not send full card details.

## 4. Situations that normally do not qualify

Subject to mandatory law, we normally do not refund solely because:
- you changed your mind after digital content was properly delivered and consumed;
- you spent, used or transferred the purchased RC or resulting virtual items;
- you dislike a later gameplay balance change that does not amount to a legal lack of conformity;
- you were warned, suspended or banned for violating server rules;
- you stopped playing or lost access to a third-party platform through no fault of RACKET;
- you purchased the wrong package after the package and price were clearly displayed.

A sanction does not cancel statutory consumer rights.

## 5. EU/EEA right of withdrawal

Consumers in the European Union or European Economic Area generally have a **14-day right of withdrawal** for many distance contracts.

For digital content supplied immediately and not on a tangible medium, that right can be lost only where the legal requirements are satisfied, including that the consumer:
1. gives prior express consent for performance to begin during the withdrawal period;
2. acknowledges that beginning performance will cause the loss of the right of withdrawal; and
3. receives the required confirmation.

RACKET will not treat the right of withdrawal as waived merely because a user clicked "Pay" or because RC was automatically delivered if the legally required consent and acknowledgement were not properly obtained.

If those conditions are not met, the consumer keeps any withdrawal/refund rights provided by applicable law.

## 6. Defective or non-conforming digital content

If purchased digital content is defective, unavailable, materially different from what was promised or otherwise non-conforming under applicable consumer law, you may be entitled to repair/correction, replacement, price reduction, termination/refund or another statutory remedy depending on the circumstances.

We may first attempt to correct delivery or restore the purchased entitlement where that is lawful and reasonable.

## 7. Refund effects

If a refund is approved:
- the refund is normally returned through the original payment method or payment provider;
- refunded RC or related virtual entitlements may be removed from the account;
- items obtained using refunded RC may also be reversed where technically possible and legally permitted;
- the transaction may be marked as refunded in our records.

The time for funds to appear depends on the payment provider and your bank or card issuer.

## 8. Chargebacks and disputes

If you believe a payment is incorrect, contact **support@racket.cat** first so we can investigate.

This does not prevent you from using any lawful dispute or chargeback rights available through your payment provider or applicable law.

Fraudulent or abusive disputes may result in account restrictions where permitted by law, but a good-faith exercise of consumer rights will not be treated as abuse.

## 9. Server sanctions

Warnings, suspensions or bans imposed for rule violations do not automatically entitle a user to reimbursement for previously delivered and conforming digital content.

However, this rule does not override mandatory refund, withdrawal or conformity rights.

## 10. Service closure or major changes

If RACKET permanently discontinues a paid service or makes a change that triggers a mandatory legal remedy for purchased digital content, we will provide the remedy required by applicable law.

Normal balancing, content rotations, economy adjustments and gameplay updates do not automatically create a refund right.

## 11. How to request a refund

Email **support@racket.cat** with:
- your RACKET username;
- the purchase/transaction reference;
- purchase date;
- package purchased;
- reason for the request;
- any relevant screenshots or evidence.

Do **not** send card numbers, CVV codes or online-banking credentials.

We may request additional information reasonably necessary to verify the purchase and prevent fraud.
