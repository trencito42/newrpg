This Privacy Policy explains how RACKET RPG ("RACKET", "we", "us") processes personal data when you use the RACKET FiveM server, **racket.cat**, the companion panel, forum, support systems and related services.

## 1. Data controller

For the Services, the data controller operates under the name **RACKET RPG**.

Privacy contact: **support@racket.cat**

The controller's complete legal identity and registered address must be added before commercial publication.

## 2. Data we process

Depending on how you use the Services, we may process:

### Account data
- username;
- email address;
- password hash and related authentication data;
- language and account preferences;
- account creation and status information.

We do not need to store your plain-text password.

### Platform and game identifiers
- Cfx.re/FiveM licence identifiers;
- Steam identifiers where available;
- Discord identifiers where available;
- identifiers associated with your account or connection.

### Gameplay and character data
- characters and character names;
- progression, XP, levels and reputation;
- inventory, vehicles, properties, businesses and virtual currency;
- factions, clans, jobs and other gameplay state;
- sanctions, warnings and moderation history;
- transaction and economy records required to operate or audit game systems.

### Website and security data
- session identifiers stored in hashed form on the server;
- IP address where the deployment is configured to record it;
- browser/user-agent information;
- login activity, session creation, expiry and revocation;
- security, anti-abuse and audit events.

### Content you submit
- forum posts and replies;
- support tickets and messages;
- complaints, appeals and evidence;
- profile information and avatars;
- screenshots, in-game phone photos and other uploaded media;
- messages or content sent through supported community features.

### Purchase and transaction data
When paid content is available, we may receive or store:
- purchase/package identifiers;
- amount, currency and status;
- transaction or checkout references;
- purchased RC or virtual items;
- refund, dispute or chargeback status.

Payment-card details are normally handled by the payment provider and should not be stored by RACKET unless expressly stated.

## 3. Where the data comes from

We receive data:
- directly from you;
- from your use of the game server and panel;
- from FiveM/Cfx.re and identifiers exposed to the server connection;
- from connected third-party services where you choose or are required to use them;
- from authorized payment providers for purchase confirmation;
- from staff moderation and support activity.

## 4. Why we use personal data

We process personal data to:
- create and authenticate accounts;
- operate gameplay, progression and persistent characters;
- maintain account and server security;
- prevent cheating, fraud, abuse and sanction evasion;
- provide support and process appeals or complaints;
- operate forums and community features;
- process and reconcile purchases;
- maintain audit trails and investigate incidents;
- save language and interface preferences;
- send service-related communications;
- comply with legal and platform obligations;
- establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

## 5. Legal bases

Where the GDPR or similar law applies, we rely on one or more of the following legal bases:

- **Contract:** processing necessary to provide the Services you request.
- **Legitimate interests:** security, fraud prevention, moderation, service integrity, abuse prevention and improvement of the Services, balanced against your rights.
- **Legal obligation:** records or disclosures required by tax, accounting, consumer, law-enforcement or other applicable law.
- **Consent:** where we specifically ask for optional consent, such as certain non-essential communications or optional technologies.

Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting earlier lawful processing.

## 6. Cookies and local preferences

The website uses essential authentication and language-preference cookies as described in the **Cookie Policy**.

RACKET currently uses the following first-party cookies in the panel:
- `sunset_panel_session` for authentication, normally up to 14 days;
- `sunset_panel_locale` for language preference, normally up to 1 year.

Additional cookies may be used by third-party sites when you leave RACKET and use their services.

## 7. Who may receive data

We may share data only where reasonably necessary with:
- hosting, infrastructure and security providers;
- email-delivery providers;
- authorized payment providers;
- staff or trusted contractors who need access to operate or secure the Services;
- professional advisers where necessary;
- public authorities or courts where legally required;
- platform operators where required to investigate abuse, enforce platform rules or comply with law.

We do not sell personal data to advertisers.

## 8. International transfers

Some providers may process data outside your country.

Where GDPR transfer rules apply, we will use an available lawful transfer mechanism, such as an adequacy decision, Standard Contractual Clauses or another mechanism permitted by law, where required.

## 9. Retention

We keep personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected and for applicable legal, security, accounting and dispute-resolution requirements.

In particular:
- active account and gameplay data may be retained while the account exists;
- web sessions expire or are revoked and may remain in security records for a limited period;
- purchase and accounting records may be retained for periods required by law;
- sanctions, fraud and security records may be retained where needed to protect the Services and prevent repeat abuse;
- support and dispute records may be retained while needed to resolve and document the matter;
- user media may remain until deleted, replaced, the related feature is removed, or retention is no longer necessary.

We may retain anonymized or aggregated information that no longer identifies you.

## 10. Security

We use technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal data, including access controls, hashed authentication/session data, restricted administrative access and audit logging.

No online system is completely risk-free. If a personal-data breach requires notice under applicable law, we will provide the required notification.

## 11. Your rights

Where the GDPR applies, you may have the right to:
- access your personal data;
- correct inaccurate data;
- request erasure;
- request restriction of processing;
- object to processing based on legitimate interests;
- receive certain data in a portable format;
- withdraw consent where processing is based on consent;
- lodge a complaint with a competent data-protection authority.

These rights may be subject to legal exceptions, including security, fraud prevention, legal claims and record-keeping duties.

For privacy requests, contact **support@racket.cat**. We may need to verify that the request relates to your account.

If you are in Romania, you may also contact the Romanian supervisory authority, ANSPDCP, where applicable.

## 12. Account deletion

You may request deletion of your account and personal data by contacting **support@racket.cat** or by using an account-deletion function if one is made available.

Deletion does not require us to erase information that must or may lawfully be retained for legal obligations, fraud prevention, security, disputes, enforcement of valid sanctions or establishment of legal claims.

Where possible, retained information will be minimized or separated from active account use.

## 13. Children and minors

The Services are not intended for anyone who is legally unable to agree to the applicable Rockstar Games, FiveM/Cfx.re or RACKET terms.

Where parental or guardian consent is legally required, the minor must not use paid or account features without that authorization.

## 14. Automated decisions

We may use automated technical rules for security, rate limiting, anti-abuse or anti-cheat detection.

Material moderation or account sanctions may involve staff review. If we introduce solely automated decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects, we will provide any notice and rights required by law.

## 15. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our systems, providers or legal obligations change.

Material changes will be communicated through an appropriate channel and the effective date will be updated.
